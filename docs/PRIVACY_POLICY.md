# Shuro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. App owner: AsHfIEXE.

Shuro is a music player for self-hosted music servers (Subsonic/OpenSubsonic
compatible, e.g. Navidrome) and for audio files stored on your device.

## Data Shuro stores on your device

- **Server connections** — server address, username and your password. The
  password is stored in Android's encrypted secure storage (Android Keystore),
  never in plain text.
- **Library life** — favourites, play history, playlists, downloads, lyrics
  cache and settings. All on this device only.
- **Playback state** — queue and position, so you can resume where you left.
- **Basic device facts** — app version, Android version, screen size and
  language. These are shown to you in Settings → About. Shuro v1 has **no
  analytics and no telemetry**: these facts are not transmitted anywhere by
  the app.

## Data Shuro sends over the network

- Playback, library and playlist requests **to the music servers you connect**
  (authenticated with your own credentials).
- Lyrics lookups to **LRCLIB** (song title and artist only) and artist
  information lookups to **MusicBrainz** (artist name only) when you use
  those features.

## What Shuro never does

- No advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs, no tracking.
- No music hosting, and no downloads from anything but your own server.
- Your server credentials are never sent to LRCLIB, MusicBrainz, or any
  third party.

## Uninstall

Uninstalling removes everything Shuro stored on the device. Your music server
and its data are never modified by Shuro, including on uninstall.
